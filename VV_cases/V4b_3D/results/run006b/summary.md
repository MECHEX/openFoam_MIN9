# V4b_3D run006b - maxCo=1.0 short smoke test

## Purpose

`run006b` is a short speed/safety smoke test for `maxCo=1.0`. It reuses the
accepted `run004b` mesh and domain (`Lin=2D`, `Lout=8D`) and runs only to
`t = 2 s`.

This is not intended as a full timestep-independence proof. The goal is to see
whether raising `maxCo` above the accepted `0.8` remains stable and gives a
similar early force response.
